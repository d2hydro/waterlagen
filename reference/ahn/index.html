    AHN

    Download hoogtegegevens met download_ahn. Kies het gebied, de AHN-versie en het hoogtemodel (dtm of dsm).

    download_ahn(
    ahn_dir=datastore.ahn_dir,
    poly_mask=None,
    select_indices=None,
    model="dtm",
    cell_size="05",
    ahn_version=4,
    service="ahn_pdok",
    missing_only=True,
    create_vrt=True,
    save_tiles_index=False,
    *,
    tile_index=None,
    retries=10,
    timeout=DEFAULT_HTTP_TIMEOUT,
)

    Download AHN rasters with validated, atomic tile replacement.

    Downloads AHN DTM or DSM rasters on 0.5 m or 5 m resolution. Each response is written to a temporary TIFF beside its target, processed, closed, reopened, and validated before it atomically replaces the final tile. A valid existing tile is reused with missing_only=True. An invalid existing tile is removed and downloaded again.

    Wat krijgt u terug?

    Type Betekenis
    Path

    Bij create_vrt=True het pad naar het VRT-mozaïek, anders de map met de gedownloade tegels.
